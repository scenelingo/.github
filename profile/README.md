# SceneLingo

**Rehearse the conversation before it counts.**

SceneLingo is a speaking practice app for people who live and work in a second language.
You understand more than you can say: SceneLingo lets you rehearse a real conversation out loud
before it happens — a job interview, an oral exam, a meeting, an appointment — with an AI partner
and feedback on what you said.

- Website: https://scenelingo.com
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6817478497
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scenelingo.app
- Also available as an app in ChatGPT

German first; English, French, Spanish, Japanese and Chinese are supported too.
